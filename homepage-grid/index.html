FAQs

Still unsure? These are the questions small companies ask us most.

Does Titan117 run on my own computer?

Yes. The agents run in the desktop app on your Windows or Mac laptop. Our cloud only handles your account, plan, credits and the relay for remote control. When the laptop is off, agents stop; scheduled work catches up on the next launch.

What do I need to install first?

Nothing. One signed installer has everything the core needs. Heavier parts, like local models or an Android emulator, download when a feature needs them, with their size shown first.

Which industries is it for?

Any small company, with ready-made workflows for retail and e-commerce, restaurants and cafés, accounting, law firms, real estate, agencies and studios, trades and construction, and wholesale and manufacturing.

What is a credit?

One credit is €0.01 of cloud model usage at our price. Every task shows its estimate first and can be capped. Local models, previews, tests and anything you run with your own API key cost no credits.

Can it act without asking me?

Not for anything external. Sending, publishing, posting and deleting always need your approval, from the app, the web, your phone or a linked chat channel.

Is there a free plan?

No. Titan117 works with a subscription from €15 a month. Local models are unlimited on every plan, so you only pay for cloud usage beyond your monthly credits.
