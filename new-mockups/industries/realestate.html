04 · Questions
Does it publish listings automatically?

Publishing is an action you approve. Titan prepares the page and posts, shows you everything, and publishes only when you say yes.

Where are buyer details kept?

In the CRM app on your laptop, in a local database. They reach our servers only if you share something.

Can it use our templates and tone?

Yes. Give Titan a few past listings and your brand kit, and it writes new listings in the same style.
