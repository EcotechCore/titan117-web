06 · Questions
What is TITAN117?

A desktop app for small companies: Titan, a general assistant, plus specialist agents for code, apps, documents, testing, marketing and automation. It runs on your own laptop.

Does my company data leave my laptop?

Agents, memory, documents and app data live on your machine. Data leaves only when you share something or send a task to a cloud model you picked.

Which models can I use?

Local models through the built-in llama.cpp, free and offline, or the strongest models through OpenRouter with our credits. You can also use your own OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google keys.

Is there a free plan or a trial?

No. Plans start at €15 a month with 700 credits, and local models are free to run on every plan.

Does it work offline?

Yes. With a signed licence, local work keeps running for 14 days without a connection, and anything that needs the internet waits in a queue.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows and macOS.
