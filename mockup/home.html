04 · Local-first

Your company stays on your laptop.

Agents, memory, documents and app data live on your machine. Data leaves only when you share something or send a task to a cloud model you picked.

05 · Pricing

Local models are free. Cloud work is credits, with the cost shown first.

1 credit is €0.01. Every task shows its estimate before it starts and can be capped.

Prices before VAT. No free plan or trial. Monthly credits roll over for one month; top-ups last 12 months.
